MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Northstar Technologies Ltd. ("Client") and Meridian Solutions Inc. ("Provider").

1. Services. The Provider shall deliver the technology services described in the applicable statements of work.

2. Fees and Payment. The Client shall pay the agreed fees in accordance with the payment schedule.

3. Indemnification. The Provider shall indemnify the Client against all losses arising from specified claims, without an express aggregate liability limit in this illustrative excerpt.

4. Renewal. The Agreement renews automatically unless written notice is provided at least sixty days before expiration.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect confidential information received from the other party.
